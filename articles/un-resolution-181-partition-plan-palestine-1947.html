UN Resolution 181 (1947) & The Partition Plan of Palestine

By Super Admin 👁 1,255 Views 📖 1 min read
On November 29, 1947, the United Nations General Assembly passed Resolution 181 by a vote of 33 to 13, recommending the partition of Mandatory Palestine into separate Jewish and Arab states, with an internationalized corpus separatum status for Jerusalem under UN administration. Despite indigenous Palestinian rejection due to allocating 56% of the land to a minority population, Resolution 181 initiated immediate military escalation and signaled the imminent end of the British Mandate on May 14, 1948.
